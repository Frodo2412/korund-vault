# Types of Quests (per region)

There are several type of quests that the party can go on throughout a region, which will have several in game effects and alter the course of further missions down the line. 

While notoriety is low at first, each quest the party embarks on makes them more of a target and they become more likely to get ambushed and attacked in a counteroffensive.
## Liberation Quest

A liberation quest consists of somehow liberating an oppressed people. This disrupts operations, and the party might be rewarded with items or allies.
## Drawing Out/Ambushing Lieutenant

This type of mission consists of drawing out or attacking a lieutenant in an advantageous position. If defeated, this lieutenant will be unavailable when raiding the palace.
## Mending breaches

Tensions between the mortal races are high. By helping out, the players can become a diplomatic force that generates alliances that extend beyond them, as they amass their own armies and allies that allow them to take the wars even furhter.
## Dungeon Crawl

Diving into ruins for secrets and items that can be helpful in the fight against the dragons. 
## Raiding the Palace

Raiding the palace means fighting the General and any available lieutenants. If sucessfull, this marks the area as liberated.
