The players wake up in the sarcophagus, deep beneath the earth. Watching over them is [[Pytheas Melikertes]], head priest of the convent. He has been waiting for them to come back, and takes their return as an omen the guardians will come back soon.

They then get introduced to the refugee camps, where they meet the other relevant NPCs. This shows them the state of the world, and how things have changed throughout the time they've been missing.

[[Dobleena Dakiti Serafatin]] speaks with them, with their power they can start designing a counter attack. She has a mission for them: a young dragon has been circling the area, no doubt looking for the refugees and hoping that finding and killing/capturing them can serve him to further develop his career. As this dragon is acting on his own, this is an opportunity to hunt a dragon that is seldom repeated.

The players then need to prepare themselves to tackle this, prepping and planning the fight and ambush. After they conquer the dragon, its essence dives deep into them, granting them further power (I will reserve the exact buff for next session, but its further progression of their dragon feat).

Other than that, during their time in town they'll meet the relevant resistance NPCs, who can help them and provide rumors and guides to other locations/dungeons where they can accomplish things and prepare for the assault on then Iron Palace.
