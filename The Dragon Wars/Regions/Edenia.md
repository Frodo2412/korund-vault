Edenia was the secret bastion and stronghold of the resistance, out of where the Metallic Dragons operated.

For the last 150 years it has been under the rule of tyrant Kaerivos Sunmark, who was rewarded with this land for killing the draconic champions. He rules with an iron fist, and takes extremely direct involvement with his land, often found out and about with his lieutenants.

# Locations in Edenia

## Refugee Camp

Located in a set of caverns beneath the valley, a small resistance still holds out. While some here push for driving the fight back to the dragon, they are a minority and the priority of the camp is to remain hidden from the dragons until the proper time to strike comes.

Important NPCs:
* [[Dobleena Dakiti Serafatin]]: Leader of the refugee camp, she's a tough [[Goliath]] fighter who found refuge in the valley as a child. Her family got killed during the attack where the dragons took over the valley at the start of the campaign. While she's now old and her fighting prowess is not what it was, she can still assist the player and provide them strategic and tactical info. 
* [[Pytheas Melikertes]]: Elvish priest that had been taking care of the temple for eons before the war got here. He's mild mannered and his priority is the wellbeing of the community. He can provide healing services to the player, as well as explaining lore and answering other questions about the world. He refuses to leave the refugees, adamant that the guardians will come back to save them and make things right. When the bastion unlocks, he takes charge of the Sacntuary.
* [[Orin Caraceniza]]: Orcish scout, he can provide knowledge about the layouts of the surrounding area and pointers on how to get to the different locations. 
* [[Sebek Tet-Uh]]: Dwarven blacksmith, he can turn rare materials into improvements or upgrades for their equipments. Once the players get their bastion, he also unlocks the Smithy bastion upgrade.
## The Iron Palace

The Iron Palace is the seat of government where [[Kaerivos Sunmark]] rules from. Storming the palace and defeating Kaerivos would make this area free once again and grant the players a bastion stronghold they can use to further their efforts, as well as a proper base of operations.
## Dragon Lab/Hatchery

With the new land, also comes a new influx of bodies to experiment on. Notable Yuan-Ti priest [[Nossu’Izumi]] has been assigned to oversee the experiments.
