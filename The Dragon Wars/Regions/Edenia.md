Edenia was the secret bastion and stronghold of the resistance, out of where the Metallic Dragons operated.

For the last 150 years it has been under the rule of tyrant Kaerivos Sunmark, who was rewarded with this land for killing the draconic champions. He rules with an iron fist, and takes extremely direct involvement with his land, often found out and about with his lieutenants.

# Locations in Edenia

## Forced work camps

The former residents of Edenia now work as slaves in the camps, they await liberation.
## The Iron Fortress

Built atop the temple of the dragons, it stands at the top of a mountain overlooking the entire valley. This is the seat of government from where Kaerivos rules.
