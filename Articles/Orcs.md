* They come from a realm of pure suffering and darkness
* They are "the ultimate life form", created by the dark lord [[Theros]] to be able to adapt and thrive in even the harshest of environments
* They are innately unmagical, being created almost as the opposite of the [[Elf]]
* They carry their culture via oral tradition, written records would hinder their nomadic culture
