* Created by performing rituals on pregnant human women with draconic flesh and body parts, which causes them to birth a dragonborn
	* It is necessary to do it this way, otherwise the dragonborn will not have a soul
	* This is a secret, the learning of which got [[Diego Fernandez]] branded as a desertor
	* Attempting similar procedures with older subjects generates defectful subjects, similar to the [[Draconian]]s from Dragonlance setting (we're using those statblocks)
* Generally built from chromatic dragons, with the single exception of the Rival Party
* They occupy the higher echelons of the military apparatus, such as generals/commanders and special units
* Due to their inherent individuality, a vestige of their human heritage, there are an abnormal number of deserters. This has caused them to be slowly phased out of the empire in favor of more reliable options.
